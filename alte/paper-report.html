2026 exam · paper & revision.

The half-yearly exam is complete. Read the actual paper with suggested answers and see what the guide missed.

What the paper actually tested

50 marks, 2 hours. Prose 20: five 1-mark facts, two 2-mark answers, two 3-mark answers, one 5-mark answer. Poetry 15: five 1-mark answers, two 2-mark answers, one 5-mark answer and two half-mark word meanings. Grammar and composition 15: five question tags, five error corrections, one 5-mark paragraph.

What the guide got right and wrong

Question-tag and common-error practice matched the two grammar tasks well. The comprehension exercises still helped reading practice, but this particular paper did not contain a comprehension section. Optional essays were useful as a form of paragraph practice, though none of the four topic titles matched exactly: “Youth Empowerment” and “Global Warming and the Future of Mankind” were close to two choices.

The biggest miss was literature. The guide emphasised summaries, themes and broad interpretations. This paper repeatedly asked for exact textbook details—Curzon Street, Dostoevsky, Kanhikuchi Airport, 55 rupees, East End, Sarnath and Bodh Gaya, the ten-year shop timeline—and word meanings. Some longer thematic questions did overlap with the guide, but not enough to make its question practice representative.

What changes next time

First extract the prescribed text chapter by chapter. Make a compact fact bank of names, places, dates, amounts, objects, sequence of events and vocabulary. Then practise the same fact in 1-, 2- and 3-mark forms; add paragraph-length answers for character, title and theme questions. Keep grammar and writing practice, but label essay topics as examples, never predictions. Use this paper as evidence of this exam—not a promise about the next paper.
