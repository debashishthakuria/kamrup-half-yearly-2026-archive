HS 2nd year · Physics

Numericals & quick checks

Six worked examples plus concept questions with hidden answers.

Worked examples · one per chapter

Try each problem without opening the solution. Use SI units and check the physical direction when relevant.

1. Two equal charges · Electric Charges & Fields

Two +2 μC point charges are 0.30 m apart. Find the force magnitude.

Show worked solution

F = (9 × 10⁹)(2 × 10⁻⁶)²/(0.30)² = 0.40 N. It is repulsive.

What does a closed Gaussian surface with no enclosed net charge tell you?
Show answer

Its net flux is zero, but the electric field at points on it need not be zero.

Why do field lines never cross?
Show answer

A crossing would give two field directions at one point, which is impossible.

2. Series capacitors · Potential & Capacitance

A 2 μF and a 3 μF capacitor are in series across 10 V. Find Q and the voltage across 2 μF.

Show worked solution

C_eq = (2×3)/(2+3) = 1.2 μF; Q = C_eqV = 12 μC. V₂ = Q/C₂ = 6 V (and V₃ = 4 V).

Can an equipotential surface cross an electric field line at an oblique angle?
Show answer

No. The field is perpendicular to an equipotential surface.

What stays constant for a capacitor disconnected from its battery?
Show answer

Charge Q (assuming no leakage); voltage can change.

3. Cell and load · Current Electricity

A 12 V cell with internal resistance 1 Ω is connected to a 5 Ω resistor. Find I and terminal voltage.

Show worked solution

I = 12/(5+1) = 2 A. Terminal voltage = 12 − 2(1) = 10 V; also IR = 10 V.

Why does a balanced Wheatstone bridge have no galvanometer current?
Show answer

Its two junctions are at equal potential.

When is terminal voltage less than emf?
Show answer

When a cell is delivering current through its internal resistance.

4. Force on a moving proton · Moving Charges & Magnetism

A proton moves at 2 × 10⁶ m/s perpendicular to a 0.5 T field. Find magnetic-force magnitude.

Show worked solution

F = evB = (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹)(2 × 10⁶)(0.5) = 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ N. Direction follows q(v × B).

Why does magnetic force not change a charged particle’s speed?
Show answer

It is perpendicular to velocity, so its instantaneous work is zero.

What is the field outside an ideal long solenoid?
Show answer

Approximately zero far from its ends.

5. Dipole torque · Magnetism & Matter

A loop has magnetic moment 0.20 A·m² in a 0.50 T field at 30° to B. Find its torque.

Show worked solution

τ = mB sin30° = (0.20)(0.50)(0.5) = 0.05 N·m.

Why do magnetic field lines have no beginning or end?
Show answer

No magnetic monopoles are observed in this model; the field has zero net closed-surface flux.

Name the three broad magnetic material classes.
Show answer

Diamagnetic, paramagnetic and ferromagnetic.

6. Changing-flux coil · Electromagnetic Induction

A 100-turn coil has flux per turn changing from 0.020 Wb to 0.005 Wb in 0.10 s. Find average emf magnitude.

Show worked solution

|ε| = 100 × |0.005 − 0.020| / 0.10 = 15 V. Direction needs the field orientation and Lenz’s law.

Why is there a minus sign in Faraday’s law?
Show answer

It encodes Lenz’s law: induced emf opposes the flux change.

Can an emf be induced without physically moving the loop?
Show answer

Yes, if the magnetic field or loop orientation/area changes so the flux changes.
