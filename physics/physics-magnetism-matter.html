Magnetism & Matter

Bar magnets, magnetic dipoles, Gauss’s law for B and magnetic materials.

NCERT Physics Part I · chapter 5 · PDF pages 177–207

ConceptFormulasDerivationsExampleQuestions

Understand the idea

A bar magnet acts as a magnetic dipole. Magnetic field lines are closed loops: there are no isolated magnetic poles in this treatment. Materials respond differently to an applied field.

Formulas · know when to use them

Magnetic dipole torque
τ=mBsin⁡θ\tau = mB\sin\thetaτ=mBsinθ
U=−m⋅BU = -\mathbf{m}\cdot\mathbf{B}U=−m⋅B
m denotes magnetic dipole moment.
Gauss’s law for magnetism
∮B⋅dA=0\oint \mathbf{B}\cdot d\mathbf{A} = 0∮B⋅dA=0
Net magnetic flux through any closed surface is zero.
Material response
M=χmHM = \chi_m HM=χm​H
B=μ0(H+M)B = \mu_0(H+M)B=μ0​(H+M)
For linear isotropic material: B = μ₀(1+χ_m)H.
Earth field components
BH=Bcos⁡δB_H = B\cos\deltaBH​=Bcosδ
BV=Bsin⁡δB_V = B\sin\deltaBV​=Bsinδ
δ is dip angle, with components in the magnetic meridian.

Magnetic Gauss law and absent monopoles (stated result)

Worked example · Dipole torque

A loop has magnetic moment 0.20 A·m² in a 0.50 T field at 30° to B. Find its torque.

τ = mB sin30° = (0.20)(0.50)(0.5) = 0.05 N·m.
