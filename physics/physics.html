Your six-chapter syllabus

Your supplied Physics syllabus: Chapters 1–6 only, from Electric Charges and Fields through Electromagnetic Induction. Paper: 50 marks. Alternating Current and Electromagnetic Waves are not included. The 70-mark ASSEB final papers below are only practice for your six chapters.

Six chapter guides

Chapter 1 · NCERT PDF pp. 5–54Electric Charges & FieldsCharge, Coulomb’s law, electric field, dipole, flux and Gauss’s law.Chapter 2 · NCERT PDF pp. 55–96Potential & CapacitancePotential, equipotentials, dipole energy, capacitors, dielectrics and stored energy.Chapter 3 · NCERT PDF pp. 97–135Current ElectricityDrift, Ohm’s law, resistivity, cells, Kirchhoff rules and Wheatstone bridge.Chapter 4 · NCERT PDF pp. 136–176Moving Charges & MagnetismLorentz force, circular motion, Biot–Savart, Ampère, parallel wires and galvanometer.Chapter 5 · NCERT PDF pp. 177–207Magnetism & MatterBar magnets, magnetic dipoles, Gauss’s law for B and magnetic materials.Chapter 6 · NCERT PDF pp. 208–236Electromagnetic InductionMagnetic flux, Faraday and Lenz, motional emf, self/mutual induction.

How to use the last hours

Choose three weak chapters for active practice; do not spend the whole night reading. Reproduce derivations without looking, solve a numerical from each important section and keep a one-page error list. The morning pass should be formulas and your mistakes, not new theory.
