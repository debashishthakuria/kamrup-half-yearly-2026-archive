What these questions are

The prompts below are faithful paraphrases of questions actually visible in the 2025 Assam HS Physics final board paper. Each includes its source PDF page and a suggested answer. The board paper has 70 marks and three hours; your local paper is 50 marks. Do not copy its section distribution.

We excluded questions outside your six prescribed chapters, including Alternating Current and Electromagnetic Waves.

2025 · 1 mark · p. 4

Moving Charges & Magnetism

An electron follows a helical path in a magnetic field. What determines its pitch?

Show suggested answer

The velocity component parallel to B sets the pitch: pitch = v_parallel T, where T = 2πm/(|q|B).

2025 · 2 marks · p. 8

Current Electricity

A 10 V battery has 3 Ω internal resistance and supplies 0.5 A. Find terminal voltage.

Show suggested answer

V = ε − Ir = 10 − (0.5)(3) = 8.5 V.

2025 · 2 marks · p. 8

Moving Charges & Magnetism

State the magnetic force on a moving charge and define one tesla.

Show suggested answer

F = q(v × B). One tesla gives a 1 N magnetic force on a 1 C charge moving at 1 m/s perpendicular to the field.

2025 · 2 marks · p. 8

Magnetism & Matter

For U = −m·B, identify a magnetic needle’s most stable and unstable orientations.

Show suggested answer

Stable: m parallel to B (minimum U = −mB). Unstable: m antiparallel to B (maximum U = +mB).

2025 · 3 marks · p. 12

Potential & Capacitance

Derive the capacitance of two large parallel plates of area A, separation d, carrying ±Q.

Show suggested answer

Neglect edges: E = Q/(ε₀A); V = Ed; C = Q/V = ε₀A/d.

2025 · 3 marks · p. 13

Moving Charges & Magnetism

State Ampère’s circuital law and use it to find B at distance r from an indefinitely long straight current I.

Show suggested answer

∮B·dl = μ₀I. Circular path gives B(2πr)=μ₀I, hence B=μ₀I/(2πr).

2025 · 3 marks · p. 14

Electromagnetic Induction

Give one distinction between an AC generator and a motor; draw a labelled AC generator.

Show suggested answer

Generator converts mechanical energy to electrical energy; motor does the reverse. Label coil, slip rings, axle and carbon brushes in your diagram.
