Moving Charges & Magnetism

Lorentz force, circular motion, Biot–Savart, Ampère, parallel wires and galvanometer.

NCERT Physics Part I · chapter 4 · PDF pages 136–176

Understand the idea

A moving charge experiences a magnetic force perpendicular to both velocity and field. A steady current creates a magnetic field; symmetry makes Ampère’s law useful for long straight conductors and solenoids.

Worked example · Force on a moving proton

A proton moves at 2 × 10⁶ m/s perpendicular to a 0.5 T field. Find magnetic-force magnitude.

F = evB = (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹)(2 × 10⁶)(0.5) = 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ N. Direction follows q(v × B).
